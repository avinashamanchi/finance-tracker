FINOVA PRIVACY

Your local ledger.

Effective draft date: August 6, 2026. This page describes the Finova iOS release candidate.

Local storage

Transactions and budgets stay in Finova's local app database unless you explicitly export or share them. Finova has no account, server API, bank connection, ads, tracking, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

User-directed files

JSON backup and CSV export create a temporary file only after your action and open the iOS share sheet. The destination you select controls its copy. Import copies a selected file into app cache, validates it, asks before replacement, and removes the temporary copy.

Deletion and backups

Delete All removes active transactions, budgets, preferences, and Finova-owned temporary artifacts from the installation. An existing iCloud or Mac/PC device backup may restore earlier data. Finova cannot inspect or remove those backups.

Security and limits

Local-only storage does not guarantee confidentiality. Device access, screenshots, shared files, malware, and unencrypted computer backups can expose entries. Finova is a record-keeping tool, not financial, tax, legal, credit, or investment advice.

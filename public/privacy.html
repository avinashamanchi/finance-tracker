FINOVA PRIVACY

Your local ledger.

Effective draft date: August 10, 2026. This page describes the Finova iOS release candidate.

Local storage

Ledger, budget, recurring-bill, and savings-goal data stays in Finova's local app database unless you explicitly export or share it. Finova has no account, server API, bank connection, ads, tracking, behavioral product-usage analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

User-directed files

Password-encrypted Finova backup and CSV export create a temporary file only after your action and open the iOS share sheet. The destination you select controls its copy. Encrypted import authenticates and validates a copied file, asks before atomic replacement, and removes the temporary copy. CSV files are not encrypted.

Finova Plus purchases

Apple processes the App Store payment. Finova does not send your ledger, amounts, descriptions, categories, backups, or support report to RevenueCat. Apple and RevenueCat process an anonymous app-user identifier, product and purchase/entitlement information, and technical request metadata to offer and restore the one-time Finova Plus purchase. Finova does not receive full payment-card details. This information is not used by Finova for advertising or tracking.

Deletion and backups

Delete All removes active finance data, preferences, and Finova-owned temporary artifacts from the installation. Finova has no user account to delete. Delete All does not cancel or refund the non-consumable, erase Apple's purchase record, or erase RevenueCat's entitlement record. Restore Purchases rechecks the current Apple account. An existing iCloud or Mac/PC device backup may restore earlier data.

Security and limits

Local-only storage and encrypted backup do not guarantee confidentiality. Device access, screenshots, shared CSV files, malware, weak or reused backup passwords, and computer backups can expose entries. Finova is a record-keeping tool, not financial, tax, legal, credit, or investment advice.

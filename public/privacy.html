FINOVA PRIVACY

Your local ledger.

Effective draft date: August 9, 2026. This page describes the Finova iOS release candidate.

Local storage

Transactions and budgets stay in Finova's local app database unless you explicitly export or share them. Finova has no account, server API, bank connection, ads, tracking, behavioral product-usage analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

User-directed files

JSON backup and CSV export create a temporary file only after your action and open the iOS share sheet. The destination you select controls its copy. Import copies a selected file into app cache, validates it, asks before replacement, and removes the temporary copy.

Finova Plus purchases

Apple processes App Store payment. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user identifier and product, purchase, entitlement, and restoration information for purchase analytics and to offer and verify the one-time Finova Plus unlock. Finova does not tie that identifier to an identity and does not send RevenueCat your ledger, budgets, exports, or full payment-card details. This information is not used for advertising or tracking.

Deletion and backups

Delete All removes active transactions, budgets, preferences, and Finova-owned temporary artifacts from the installation. Finova has no user account to delete. Removing local data or the app does not revoke the Apple purchase record or RevenueCat entitlement; Restore Purchases can recover the unlock for the same Apple account. An existing iCloud or Mac/PC device backup may restore earlier data.

Security and limits

Local-only storage does not guarantee confidentiality. Device access, screenshots, shared files, malware, and unencrypted computer backups can expose entries. Finova is a record-keeping tool, not financial, tax, legal, credit, or investment advice.

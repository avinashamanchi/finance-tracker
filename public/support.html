FINOVA SUPPORT

Know what the numbers mean.

Finova is a private record-keeping and visualization tool. It is not financial, tax, legal, credit, or investment advice.

Totals look wrong

Review transaction type, amount, date, and category. Missing or incorrect entries change every dashboard, budget, and insight calculation.

Budget status

Budgets compare recorded expenses with the limit you set. They do not block a purchase, inspect a bank account, predict a bill, or move money.

Import and deletion

Import validates a Finova JSON backup before asking to replace active data. Finova cannot recover deleted data. An older device backup may restore earlier entries.

Export status

A share sheet closing does not prove another app saved or delivered the file. Check the destination you selected. Finova removes its temporary export after the sheet closes.

Restore Finova Plus

In Finova Settings, open View Finova plans and choose Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple account that bought the one-time unlock. Restoring Plus does not restore deleted ledger entries; use your own JSON or device backup for finance data.

Apple purchase help

For an App Store charge or refund request, use Apple purchase support. Apple decides refund eligibility under its policies. Finova Plus is a one-time purchase and does not renew.
